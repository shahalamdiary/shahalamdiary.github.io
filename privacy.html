Privacy Policy

My Diary

Last updated: November 21, 2026

My Diary is a personal diary application designed to help users write, save, organize and protect their personal memories. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the application may handle the following information:

  • Account information, such as your email address, for authentication.
  • Diary titles, dates, notes and other entries you create.
  • Photos that you choose to attach to diary entries.
  • Profile information and profile photos that you provide.

2. Local Storage

Diary entries and related information may be stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This allows you to access your diary entries on your device, including when an internet connection is unavailable.

3. Firebase Services

My Diary uses Firebase Authentication for account sign-in and Firebase Cloud Firestore for synchronizing supported diary information with your account.

Information synchronized through Firebase is associated with your account and is used to provide the application's account and synchronization features.

4. Optional Google Drive Backup

My Diary may offer optional Google Drive backup and restore features for supported photos and profile information. These features are used only when you choose to authorize and use Google Drive.

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission allows the application to access files that it creates or that you explicitly open or share with the application, rather than providing general access to your entire Drive.

Google Drive data is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security policies.

5. Data Sharing

My Diary does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services described in this policy, such as Firebase and Google Drive, when required to provide the features you use.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect information through the security features provided by the application and its supporting services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Diary information may remain on your device or in the connected services until it is deleted through the application's available features or the relevant service. Removing the application from your device may not delete information already synchronized to cloud services.

If you need assistance regarding your account or personal information, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

My Diary is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
